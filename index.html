Being a cabbie

Look for people with their arm up (🟡 on the map). Pull up beside them and stop.

On a phone: put a finger anywhere on the left side of the screen and slide it left/right to steer, hold GO / BRAKE with your other thumb, and tap the big yellow button when it appears (let passengers in, start/stop the meter…). Everything else is in the ☰ CAB menu.

L unlock / lock the doors to let them in or out
M start the meter (don't forget!) and stop it on arrival
1/2 reply when passengers chat
Tab map · F charge at ⚡ · Esc pause

Payment: use the card terminal (key in the fare, pass it through) or count out change from your money bag.

We drive on the left. Red lights and speed cameras will fine you. Smooth driving = better tips.
